I am a postdoctoral researcher at the Broad Institute of MIT and Harvard.

I completed my Ph.D. at Harvard University co-advised by Drs. Mark Daly and Steve McCarroll, and continued in their groups to extend that work. 

I study how missense variation causes neuropsychiatric and neurodevelopmental disease, and I'm particularly interested in using missense variation to 
discover novel biology. 

My current work develops structure-based approaches for interpreting rare coding variation. asking not just which genes carry disease risk, 
but where in the folded protein that risk concentrates and what it does there.
